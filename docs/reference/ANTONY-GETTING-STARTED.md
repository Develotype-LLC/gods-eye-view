## Explore the map

- Choose **Permian**, **Texas**, or **US basins** across the top.
- Use **Overview**, **Water**, **Ground movement**, or the individual layer switches.
- Zoom in and click mapped records to inspect their source and details.
- Use **Land ownership** to search recorded owners, select owner classes or client
  portfolios, and inspect parcel boundaries. Imported Texas ownership coverage is
  focused on the Permian and Palo Duro basins; it is not statewide ownership coverage.

## Compare a produced-water pipeline corridor

1. Open **LONG-Haul · pipeline routing**.
2. Start with **Load Permian example**, or pick source A and delivery B on the map.
3. Add required waypoints or draw exclusion areas if needed.
4. Choose whether to prefer existing mapped pipeline corridors and review crossing
   penalties. These preferences are assumptions, not construction quotations.
5. Under **Operating assumptions**, pick a scenario. Expand **View or customize
   assumptions** to change flow, diameter, fluid properties or electricity cost.
6. Choose **Find route alternatives** and wait for the comparison to finish.
7. Select an alternative to inspect its alignment, terrain profile, pumping estimate
   and parcel/owner list.

Compare **known names**, **parcels**, and **unresolved** separately. Several parcels
can share an owner; several unknown-owner parcels do not constitute one owner.
Missing ownership or data coverage prevents an automatic recommendation.

**Save draft here** saves inputs only in the current browser. **Export selected
route** creates a GeoJSON file with the candidate geometry and assumptions for
sharing; it does not include private relationship notes.

## Owner relationships

Owner-profile buttons and **Owners & relationships** use project-scoped access.
Antony’s initial login has no private relationship project assigned. That section
will show an unassigned-project message until Brian approves membership. Once
assigned, it supports contacts, relationship notes, transaction-specific willingness
assessments, next actions and edit history. Follow-up dates are worklist entries,
not automatic reminders.

## How to interpret results

- Appraisal-reported owner names are not verified legal identity, title, mineral
  rights, or permission to build. Surface and mineral interests are distinct.
- A mapped pipeline does not establish operating capacity or access to its corridor.
- Preset flows are screening scenarios, not rated capacities.
- Pumping currently models **one pipe per route**, not parallel lines or station
  sizing. Electricity estimates exclude construction, easements and maintenance.
- Treat alternatives as candidates for investigation and engineering review.

When reporting an issue, include the chosen endpoints, operating scenario, selected
alternative and a screenshot. Avoid including passwords or private contact notes.
